Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information the AU Gate Discord bot (the “bot”, “we”, “us”) collects when you join or use the IamKing Community server, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It applies only to the bot and the server it runs in.

Operator contact: [add a contact email or Discord handle — e.g. the server owner]. Replace this placeholder before relying on this policy.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to members of the Discord server where AU Gate is active. If you do not wish to have the data described below processed, do not join the server (or leave it and request deletion, see section 8).

2. What we collect

When you interact with the bot, we may store the following:

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Discord user ID and username Your Discord account To identify you, assign roles, and match against the index.
Among Us friend code(s) What you submit via the verification form To gate access and detect ban evasion, since friend codes are permanent.
Join / verification timestamps and status Your activity in the server To run the verification timeout and reminders.
Report contents (category, description, evidence links, reporter ID) Submitted by members via /report To let moderators review reports of misconduct.
Index entries (friend code and/or Discord ID, reason, evidence) Moderator actions and approved reports To block known bad actors and their linked accounts.
Audit log (actions such as verify, kick, ban, index changes) Bot activity To keep a moderation record and allow appeals.

The bot does not read your private messages, does not collect your email or payment details, and does not use advertising or tracking cookies on these web pages.

3. Why we process it

  • To operate the membership gate and grant the correct roles.
  • To keep the community safe from reported and repeat bad actors (our legitimate interest).
  • To let moderators review reports and handle appeals.
  • To comply with Discord’s and applicable platform rules.

4. Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a private database on a self-hosted server operated by the community. It is not sold or rented. Access is limited to the bot’s operators and the server’s moderators. Summary information (for example “this user was verified” or “this user was banned”) may be posted to the server’s private moderator channels.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing. Data is processed through Discord as part of normal bot operation and is therefore also subject to Discord’s own Privacy Policy. If the community later links this bot with other servers, this policy will be updated first and the change announced (see section 10).

6. How long we keep it

  • Verification and membership records: while you are a member and for a reasonable period after.
  • Index entries: retained while they are considered necessary for community safety.
  • Reports and audit logs: retained for moderation record-keeping and appeals.

You can ask for your data to be removed at any time (section 8).

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data (private hosting, restricted access, secure tokens). No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not share your Discord token or password with anyone, including moderators.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may contact the operator (section 12) to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data.
  • Object to processing.

We will respond within a reasonable time. Some records — in particular index entries and audit logs — may be kept, in whole or in part, where retaining them is necessary to keep the community safe or to comply with the law. Where that happens, we will explain why.

9. Children

The server and bot are intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum age requirement (13+, or higher where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the server, and the effective date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the server after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. International users

The bot is operated from the operator’s location, and your data may be processed there. By using the server you understand this transfer may occur.
