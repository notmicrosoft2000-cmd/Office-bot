Office Bot

Among Us friend-code verification and moderation for IamKing Community (anti predators). Office#6118

Add to server Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Office Bot gates new members by their Among Us friend code. Friend codes are permanent and account-bound — players cannot change them — so they survive Discord account changes and make a genuinely useful ban-evasion tripwire.

What it does
  • Join gate — new members get an AU Unverified role and can only see the verify channel until they pass.
  • Friend-code capture — a code is checked for the right format, normalised, and stored, linked to the account that sent it.
  • Moderator-approved index — a list of friend codes and Discord IDs tied to reported misconduct. Only reports a moderator approves are added.
  • Ban-evasion detection — if a code is indexed, every Discord account ever linked to it is banned on sight.
  • Opt-out — members who genuinely don't play Among Us can say so instead of verifying.
  • Reminder DMs — a friendly nudge at the start, halfway point, and final deadline.

Not affiliated. Office Bot is a community-run tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Discord Inc. or Innersloth LLC.

Verification, step by step

  1. You join the server and receive the AU Unverified role. While unverified, the verify channel is the only place you can see.
  2. You press the button (or run /verify) and submit your Among Us friend code — it looks like Name#1234.
  3. The code is checked against the index. A hit means an immediate ban, and any other account linked to that code is banned too.
  4. No hit? You're verified, the role is swapped, and the rest of the server opens up.
  5. Rather not play? Choose “I don't play Among Us” and you're let through as a non-player.

If you don't verify in time

Office Bot DMs you a reminder at the start, halfway, and at the final deadline. Miss all three and you're removed from the server so it can be rejoined later.

Reporting a friend code

Anyone can report an Among Us friend code with /report — type the code (Example#0000) and pick a reason: predatory behaviour, inappropriate sexual behaviour, harassment, hate speech, scamming or ban evasion. You then describe what happened and can attach evidence. Reports go to a private moderator queue and only reach the index once a moderator approves them — so a single player can't get someone banned on their own.

Don't submit someone else's friend code. Framing another player breaks the Terms of Service and can get you indexed yourself.
